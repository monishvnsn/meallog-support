Privacy at MealLog

A food journal for your iPhone and iPad.

Last updated October 17, 2026

Information on your device

Guest journal entries, food descriptions, dates and times, reference nutrition, optional photos, drafts and display preferences are stored on your device. MealLog does not upload a guest journal. Exports are created locally and shared only through the destination you choose.

Sign in and optional encrypted sync

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple authenticates your account. The app stores Apple's stable account identifier and any email Apple shares in the device Keychain; email is not included in meal records or exports. When encrypted sync is enabled, the app encrypts each saved meal record on the device with AES-GCM before sending it to Supabase. The service stores the encrypted payload, opaque meal identifier, revision and account identifier needed to retrieve the journal. Meal descriptions, dates, nutrition values, photos, drafts and preferences are not stored as readable database fields. A random encryption key is kept in synchronizable iCloud Keychain so your Apple devices can open encrypted records after sign-in. Photos, drafts and preferences stay on the device.

The sync service cannot decrypt meal content from the stored ciphertext. Account identifiers, connection metadata and encrypted records are processed by Supabase under its terms and privacy policy. Sync is unavailable in builds where the service is not configured. Apple authentication works independently of sync.

Barcode lookups and external links

When you scan a packaged-food barcode, its product code is sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve public product and nutrition details. The provider and network infrastructure may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and the app's public User-Agent. Meal photos and journal records are not sent to this provider. Local reference-food searches use a bundled USDA catalog. Links that you open are governed by the destination's own privacy policy.

Nutrition information

Nutrition values are reference estimates per 100 g or 100 mL. They do not measure the amount eaten and are not medical advice. Values depend on the quality and coverage of the source data.

Retention, export and deletion

You can export saved meals in Settings. Guests can erase the local journal. Signed-in users can delete synced journal records and local journal data in Settings; deletion closes the sync journal to stale devices. Account deletion and Apple authorization revocation must be available before public launch. Use Apple Account Settings to manage or revoke Sign in with Apple access.

Providers and tracking

MealLog has no advertising or tracking SDK and does not use paid AI analysis. Apple provides authentication and iCloud Keychain key recovery; Supabase provides optional account and encrypted-journal sync; Open Food Facts provides barcode product data. Nutrition descriptions and photos are not sent for AI analysis in this version.
