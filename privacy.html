Privacy at MealLog

A food journal for your iPhone and iPad.

Last updated October 17, 2026

Information on your device

Guest journal entries, food descriptions, dates and times, reference nutrition, optional photos, drafts and display preferences are stored on your device. MealLog does not upload a guest journal. Exports are created locally and shared only through the destination you choose.

Sign in and iCloud sync

If you choose Sign in with Apple, the app uses Apple authentication. The Apple account identifier and any email Apple shares are stored in this device's Keychain; the email is excluded from meal exports and meal records. Saved meals, dates and times, notes, source information, available nutrition and deletion state sync to your private iCloud account through Apple's private CloudKit database. MealLog does not operate a journal server. Photos, unfinished drafts and preferences remain on each device.

Barcode lookups and external links

When you scan a packaged-food barcode, its product code is sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve public product and nutrition details. The provider and network infrastructure may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and the app's public User-Agent. Meal photos and journal records are not sent to this provider. Local reference-food searches use a bundled USDA catalog. Links that you open are governed by the destination's own privacy policy.

Nutrition information

Nutrition values are reference estimates per 100 g or 100 mL. They do not measure the amount eaten and are not medical advice. Values depend on the quality and coverage of the source data.

Retention, export and deletion

You can export saved meals in Settings. Guests can erase the local journal. Signed-in users can delete their private CloudKit journal and local account data in Settings. A minimal private cloud control marker remains to prevent an offline device from restoring deleted meals. Other devices may retain local copies until they reconnect or their local app data is removed. Use Apple Account Settings to revoke Sign in with Apple authorization.

Providers and tracking

MealLog has no developer-operated meal database, advertising SDK, tracking SDK, paid AI analysis, or Google sign-in. Apple provides authentication and CloudKit; Open Food Facts provides barcode product data.
