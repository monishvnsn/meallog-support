MealLog Support

A little help for your food journal.

Contact

For help with MealLog, email appsupport@duck.com. Please include the app version, device and iOS version, what you tapped, and whether the issue repeats.

Please avoid sending meal records or photos unless you choose to share them. For a nutrition mismatch, include the public food reference or product link.

Common questions

  • Why is a meal unrated? Some reference foods have incomplete or conflicting data. Search for a closer match; unknown values are not treated as zero.
  • What do nutrition values mean? They describe a matched reference per 100 g or 100 mL, not the amount eaten.
  • Does barcode lookup always work? New lookups need internet access, and some products are not in the public catalog. You can still save a text entry.
  • Where is my journal stored? Guest meals stay on your device. If you sign in with Apple, saved meals sync to your private iCloud account. Photos, drafts and preferences stay on each device.
  • Can I export or delete my journal? Use Settings in the app. Export before deletion if you need a copy.
